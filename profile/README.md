## About Us

NoMercy Entertainment is a passionate team dedicated to revolutionizing media consumption through open-source innovation. Founded by Stoney Eagle, we focus on empowering individuals to own, manage, and stream their media collections with unparalleled privacy and ease. Our mission is to break free from the constraints of traditional streaming services, providing tools that prioritize user ownership, data privacy, and community-driven features.

We believe in making the internet a better place by fostering creativity, collaboration, and accessibility in media technology. Whether you're a content creator, a media enthusiast, or someone who values digital freedom, NoMercy Entertainment is here to support your journey.

## What We Do

We develop comprehensive, open-source solutions for media management and streaming:

- **NoMercy MediaServer**: The Effortless Encoder – A powerful media server for encoding, organizing, and streaming your personal media library with privacy and simplicity.
- **NoMercy App**: A user-friendly TV app for seamless media consumption.
- **Media Players**: Headless, event-driven players for video and music, including Chromecast integration.
- **Supporting Technologies**: Custom tools for media processing and infrastructure.

Our projects span multiple programming languages including C#, TypeScript, JavaScript, Vue, and more, reflecting our commitment to robust, cross-platform solutions.

## Our Mission

To empower media ownership and overcome licensing barriers by providing privacy-focused, user-centric tools that make archiving and streaming personal content as effortless as using commercial services. We stand against digital restrictions, advocating for open access to your media without DRM or vendor lock-in.

## Key Principles

- **Privacy First**: Your data stays yours – no tracking, no DRM.
- **Open Source**: Transparency and community collaboration.
- **Innovation**: Pushing boundaries in media technology.
- **Community**: Building tools that connect and enhance user experiences.

## Our Ecosystem

The NoMercy MediaServer ecosystem is a suite of interconnected tools designed for complete media ownership and streaming. Here's an overview of our key repositories:

### Core Components
- [**NoMercyMediaServer**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyMediaServer)  
  The flagship media server for encoding, managing, and streaming personal media collections with a focus on privacy and ease of use.
- [**NoMercyApp**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyApp)  
  A sleek TV application for consuming media from your NoMercy server.
- [**NoMercyVideoPlayer**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyVideoPlayer)  
  An event-driven, headless video player for seamless playback.
- [**NoMercyMusicPlayer**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyMusicPlayer)  
  A headless music player designed for background audio streaming.
- [**NoMercyCastPlayer**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyCastPlayer)  
  Chromecast integration for casting music from the NoMercyMusicPlayer.

### Supporting Tools
- [**MediaSession**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/MediaSession)  
  Media Session API implementation for Capacitor and web platforms.
- [**NoMercyStack**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyStack)  
  Docker-based infrastructure setup for deploying NoMercy services.
- [**NoMercy.tv**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercy.tv)  
  The official website and community hub.
- [**Examples**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/Examples)  
  Library examples and usage guides for developers.

### Media Processing
- [**NoMercyFFMpeg**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyFFMpeg)  
  Custom FFmpeg configurations for advanced media processing.
- [**NoMercyTesseract**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/NoMercyTesseract)  
  OCR capabilities using Tesseract for media metadata extraction.
- [**whisper.cpp**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/whisper.cpp)  
  Backbone for model integration, enabling advanced audio processing and transcription features within the media server using FFmpeg.

### Infrastructure
- [**Github-Runner-Docker**](https://github.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/Github-Runner-Docker)  
  Dockerized GitHub Actions runners for CI/CD pipelines.

All our repositories are open-source and welcome contributions. Check out the individual READMEs for detailed setup and usage instructions.
